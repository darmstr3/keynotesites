Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Do I need to do the fit check first?

No, it's optional. It's a short call to confirm this is the right scope for your site before any money changes hands, not a sales pitch. If you already know you want in, skip straight to the deposit.

What happens to my current site and hosting?

Your new site replaces it, and redirects are set up so your Google standing carries over. If you're currently paying a monthly platform fee (Wix, Squarespace, etc.), that goes away once you're moved over.

What counts as a revision round?

One consolidated list of changes per round, two rounds included. Send everything at once and I make it all. Additional rounds are quoted separately.

What happens after the first year of hosting?

It renews at $149/yr. You can cancel any time and take your code with you, this is never an open-ended obligation on either side.

What's the refund policy?

Your 50% deposit is fully refundable any time before you approve the preview, no questions asked. Once you approve it, that deposit covers the work already delivered. The remaining 50% is only ever charged once you're happy with the site and it's ready to launch, so you're never billed for something you haven't signed off on.
