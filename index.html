The problem

Planners decide in about 90 seconds.

Before anyone books you, someone checks your website. Most speaker sites lose the booking in three ways:

The showreel is buried

Video is the first thing a planner looks for and the last thing most sites deliver. If they can't watch you speak in one scroll, they move to the next name on their list.

The talks read like course catalogs

Keynote descriptions written for you, not for the person hiring you. Planners need titles, outcomes, and who it's for. In seconds, not paragraphs.

Booking takes work

A 12-field form, a dead email link, or no clear next step at all. Every extra click between "impressed" and "inquired" costs you real gigs.

Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?

One-page structure, one proven design system, one revision round, structured intake instead of endless meetings. The parts that make custom projects cost $5,000 are the parts that make them slow. I removed those, not the design quality.

When does the 3-day clock start?

When your intake form is complete: bio, talks, photos, video links, domain details. Most speakers finish it in under 30 minutes. If assets trickle in over two weeks, the clock waits with them.

What counts as the revision round?

One consolidated list of changes after you see the preview. Send everything at once and I make it all. Additional rounds are $75. Layout and design-system changes are custom work, which starts at $1,500 and is usually not what you need.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Content updates are handled for you: two per month on the Care Plan, or $75 per batch without it. A self-serve content editor is on the roadmap for Care Plan members. What you can't do is break the design at 1am. That's a feature.

I already have a website. What happens to it?

I replace it rather than renovate it. Your existing content actually speeds up the build, and I set up redirects from your old pages so links and Google rankings carry over.

What do you need from me?

About 25 minutes on the intake form: bio, talk descriptions, testimonials, a headshot and speaking photos, video links, and access to point your domain. I shape everything from there.

What's not included?

Logo design, ghostwriting from scratch, blogs, email marketing, and ongoing SEO campaigns. Keeping the box small is what keeps it $499 and 3 days.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund any time before you approve your preview. After launch, the build is complete and non-refundable. The Care Plan can be cancelled anytime.
